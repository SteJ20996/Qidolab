Where these puzzles come from

Puzzle roots & our approach

  • Fox search: Rustan Leino’s puzzle collection (credits Claude Marché for a related form).
  • River crossing: MacTutor on Alcuin’s puzzle book (a historical account of the river puzzle).
  • Tower of Hanoi: MacTutor history of Édouard Lucas (Lucas, 1883).
  • Queens: V. Chvátal’s eight queens history (first posed by Bezzel in 1848).

We adapted only the puzzle mechanics. The teaching text, visuals and code on this page were made for Qidolab; these links document earlier forms of the ideas.
